# Market Research Report: An Online Multiplayer Rust Game Built by One Founder

Prepared 2026-10-05 for the founder, a senior backend and data engineer new to game development and to Rust; revised 2026-10-07 after a critic review and five gap-filling research passes (listed under "Reviewer gaps addressed" at the end of Section 8). Numbers first; every figure carries a link, and self-computed catalog figures now link to the exact dump release they were computed from; estimator outputs, search-snippet figures and unverified figures are flagged.

## 1. Executive summary

Build **Longthaw, staged**: a player-hosted 1-4 player scavenging game with a settlement that keeps working between sessions, set in a county that thaws week by week, sold on Steam at $17.99-19.99, with the Rust authoritative server shipped as a listen server inside the client plus a free headless dedicated binary over Steam Datagram Relay. The concept survives the critic review; the pitch's calendar, its comp set, two of its headline statistics and two of its platform assumptions do not, and the recommendation below is re-derived from the corrected evidence rather than carried over.

**What changed.** (1) The lane is occupied, not empty. Project Zomboid shipped Build 42 multiplayer to stable on [2026-07-29](https://nichegamer.com/project-zomboid-breaks-steam-record-after-build-42-stable-launch/) and set a [121,603 Steam peak](https://nichegamer.com/project-zomboid-breaks-its-steam-record-again-with-over-121000-players/) at a new $24.99 price; HumanitZ, the closest shipped product to Longthaw's shape, left Early Access on [2026-02-06](https://www.indie.io/post/sandbox-zombie-survival-humanitz-exits-steam-early-access-on-feb-6) and sits at ~13,600 reviews at 74% with an est. [$2.9M, a publisher and ~410k wishlists](https://raijin.gg/app/1766060/HumanitZ); the only solo entrant in this exact slot, [Survivalist: Invisible Strain, took five years of EA](https://automaton-media.com/articles/newsjp/survivalist-invisible-strain-20250426-336652/) to reach [3,007 reviews and a 795-player peak](https://www.steamspy.com/app/1054510). The realistic outcome distribution is Survivalist/CryoFall-shaped (low thousands of reviews, $1-2M over three to five years) with HumanitZ as the ceiling, not Duckov-shaped. (2) The base rate for Longthaw's own tag set is [10.6% at 1,000 reviews (5/47, paid 2022-24) and 2.9% self-published (1/34)](https://github.com/leinstay/steamdb/releases/latest/download/steamdb.min.json.gz), not the 20.8% OWSC figure, which the same dump shows is a 3D first/third-person number; self-published 3D survival co-op runs at 17.6%, which turns the camera into a commercial decision. (3) The co-op multiplier is real but about half composition: 8.4x raw at 5,000 reviews, 3.0-4.4x after controlling for price, publisher, developer history and year, 2-3x inside this sub-genre. (4) A title may enter [exactly one Steam Next Fest](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/upcoming_events/nextfest), prologues are ineligible and Early Access counts as released, so the plan's two Next Fests and free Prologue app collapse to one demo and one fest: February 2028 by default, October 2027 as a withdrawable option. (5) A headless Rust dedicated server over SDR exists off the shelf: [steamworks-rs has exposed GameServer networking sockets since PR #249](https://github.com/Noxime/steamworks-rs/pull/249) and [aeronet_steam accepts a Server handle since PR #94](https://github.com/aecsocket/aeronet/pull/94) (release-candidate only), while renet_steam and renet2_steam take a Client handle only and need a 20-40 line patch; Gap #2 is days of glue plus a mandatory one-day spike, not 100-300 hours, and the exemplar is Core Keeper, not Valheim. (6) The hours line was wrong: a 45-50 hour cap with a day off and deloads yields 2,025-2,250 logged hours, not 2,400-2,560 productive, and the bottom-up cost of the Q1-Q3 scope as pitched is ~2,250 likely founder-hours against ~1,750-1,950 available by month 9, so the ~2,000-hour build is the base case and the full pitch is the upside. 58 hours a week is 3,000 hours only with zero weeks off, and no comp in this report sustained it.

**What stays.** Co-op survival remains the strongest lane on Steam in 2026 ([a survival game has sat near the top of the charts every year for five years and 2026 did not break the streak](https://cogconnected.com/2026/07/co-op-survival-games-wont-stop-eating-steams-charts/)); after controls, online co-op is still the largest controllable multiplier in the catalog; the player-hosted model still has no hosting bill, no population floor and no anti-cheat burden; and in the founder's own stratum (self-published, first title, online co-op, $15-19.99, all genres) the dump shows [18.1% at 1,000 reviews and 10.2% at 5,000](https://github.com/leinstay/steamdb/releases/latest/download/steamdb.min.json.gz). The year-one deliverable is a live Steam page, a public 30-90 minute demo and a wishlist pile; EA ships inside the ~30-day halo after the chosen Next Fest at 25-30k wishlists or with a publisher, realistically March-May 2028. What the skeptics changed in year-one arithmetic stands: at a 15k-wishlist gate, published conversion rates yield roughly [$50k gross](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/the-state-of-steam-wishlist-conversions), not $100k, and in this lane dollars per review run $213-396, so a Survivalist-shaped 3,000-review outcome is $0.6-1.2M lifetime over several years.

## 2. Founder profile, taste DNA, and the budget correction

**Profile.** Solo developer plus AI coding agents; 25 years of backend and data engineering; new to Rust and to game development; 12-month horizon. Hard constraints: online multiplayer from day one with a Rust authoritative server; buildable by one person plus agents; no art team; commercial viability drives design; learn Rust deeply.

**Taste DNA.** Fallout, Rise of Kingdoms, The Division and SimCity are four wrappers around one loop: build a home base, go out into a systemic world to gather, come back and compound. Pillars: the base as anchor of identity (FO4 settlements, FO76 CAMPs, RoK city); legible systems (GlassBox agents, junk-to-components, explicit gear math); months-long compounding progression; the out-and-back expedition; solo-viable play with a social multiplier; bounded opt-in stakes (Dark Zone, KvK); post-collapse worlds told through environment. Session shape: 2-10 minute async check-ins, 30-90 minute runs, occasional 2-4 hour deep sessions. Dislikes: timer-gating and pay-to-skip, aim-skill PvP as the core, full-loot open PvP, heavy authored narrative, always-online shipped before the server is ready, wipes, content-treadmill live service. Quantic Foundry placement: Mastery-Achievement and Immersion-Creativity bridged by Discovery; Action-Social is weakest.

**Budget correction.** The profile said 20 hours per week (~1,000 hours per year); the founder has clarified ~3,000 hours per year, about 58 per week. Consequences: (a) at 1,000 hours the honest answer was "pick one half of the taste"; at 3,000 the second pass judged that both halves (persistent shared world plus embodied co-op run) fit one staged game inside 15-18 months, so the merged pitches rose to the top, a judgement the post-review arithmetic in (f) narrows to the embodied half in year one and the shared-world half in years two to three; (b) agents multiply throughput on server, persistence, protocol, tests and tooling, and barely help with game feel, combat tuning, shaders, asset integration and Bevy churn, so the schedule is bounded by roughly 1,200-1,500 founder-only hours; (c) the only randomized trial of experienced developers using AI tools found them [19% slower while believing they were 20% faster](https://metr.org/blog/2025-07-10-early-2025-ai-experienced-os-dev-study/), and the [2026 follow-up's +18% carries a -9% to +38% interval](https://birchtree.me/blog/an-update-from-the-study-that-said-devs-were-actually-slower-with-coding-agents/), so the pitches' 2-3x multiplier is an assumption; (d) 58 hours sits above the [~50-hour point where output per hour falls](https://docs.iza.org/dp8129.pdf) (not fetched, medium confidence) and inside the [55-hour band WHO/ILO associate with +35% stroke risk](https://www.who.int/news/item/17-05-2021-long-working-hours-increasing-deaths-from-heart-disease-and-stroke-who-ilo); (e) Steam overhead still costs 4-6 weeks; (f) the hours themselves were mis-stated: 3,000 hours a year is 57.7 hours a week with no time off, or 61.2 over 49 working weeks, while a 45-50 hour cap with a weekly day off, two weeks off, a sick week and a half-hours deload every sixth week yields 2,025-2,250 logged hours (~1,600-1,950 productive at 80-85%, an analyst share), and [only a third of Google's experienced engineers matched their prior-language productivity in Rust within two months, just over half by four](https://opensource.googleblog.com/2023/06/rust-fact-vs-fiction-5-insights-from-googles-rust-journey-2022.html) (search summary of the primary page, medium-high), a 180-300 hour ramp tax on top.

## 3. Market landscape

Caveat for every lane: Steam, SteamDB, Gamalytic, VG Insights, Sensor Tower, GameDiscoverCo and most press sites were egress-blocked, so many figures come from search snippets, GitHub-hosted estimator CSVs and a nightly Steam catalog dump ([leinstay/steamdb](https://github.com/leinstay/steamdb), ~190k rows, 2026-10-04/06). Developer-announced milestones are high confidence; estimator revenues are medium at best and diverge up to 2x between tools (Enshrouded [$119.5M on Sensor Tower](https://app.sensortower.com/vgi/game/enshrouded) vs ~$53M elsewhere). Self-computed catalog figures link to the [dump release they were computed from](https://github.com/leinstay/steamdb/releases/latest/download/steamdb.min.json.gz) (generated 2026-10-06, 187,815 Steam rows; paid = list price of $0.99 or more; year = earliest of release, store and Early Access date); the earlier placeholder links to the steamdb.info and vginsights.com homepages have been replaced. Tag coverage in the dump is 74% (2022), 69% (2023), 28% (2024) and 1.2% (2025), tagged rows are success-biased (tagged 2025 paid titles reach 1,000 reviews 21% of the time against 2.5% untagged) and tags are capped at about 20 per title, so tag-based rates use 2022-24 only and 2025 rates use store categories, which have full coverage. Secondary sources are labelled as such: GDC talks are quoted from a [community transcript mirror](https://github.com/dklassic/GDC-transcript), Gamalytic figures from student-repository scrapes, GameDiscoverCo and Zukowski analyses often through wnhub, presskit.gg, cinevva or steampageanalyzer relays, and Steam review counts from Raijin, steambase, SteamSpy or vaporlens snapshots that disagree with each other by date; the two-day re-verification task in Section 7 exists for these.

## 4. The gaps we found (ranked)

1. **Online co-op top-down PvE scavenging with a settlement layer: an occupied lane, not a gap (revised).** The nearest shipped products, absent from the pitch, are [Project Zomboid](https://raijin.gg/app/108600/Project_Zomboid) (B42 multiplayer stable 2026-07-29, 121,603 peak, ~449k reviews at 94%, $24.99), [HumanitZ](https://raijin.gg/app/1766060/HumanitZ) (1.0 on 2026-02-06, 12,674 peak, ~13,600 reviews at 74%, est. $2.9M with a publisher), [Survivalist: Invisible Strain](https://www.steamspy.com/app/1054510) (solo, five years of EA, 3,007 reviews at 88%, 795 peak), [CryoFall](https://www.steampulse.org/game/829590) (five developers, Daedalic, 6,414 reviews, est. $2.2M over seven years), [7 Days to Die](https://thefunpimps.com/) (20M+) and [State of Decay 2](https://raijin.gg/app/495420/State_of_Decay_2) (69,487 reviews; host-only co-op called "barebones"). The tag set's base rate is [10.6% at 1,000 reviews, 2.9% self-published, 6.8% on the wide 2D definition](https://github.com/leinstay/steamdb/releases/latest/download/steamdb.min.json.gz); online co-op lifts the 5,000-review rate 8.4x raw and 3.0-4.4x after controls, 2-3x inside this sub-genre. What is unsupplied is the narrow shape: a small-team, PvE-only co-op scavenger with a persistent settlement and a calendar hook; that is absence of supply, not proof of demand. The [ZERO Sievert](https://underdog.ltd/presskit.html) and [Duckov](https://raijin.gg/app/3167020/Escape_From_Duckov) "no multiplayer" argument has expired: Duckov averaged ~3,855 daily players by July 2026 against a 301,244 peak and its Workshop co-op mod is a 2-player beta Team Soda will not productize. Realistic distribution: Survivalist/CryoFall-shaped median ($1-2M lifetime over three to five years), HumanitZ-shaped ceiling with a publisher and ~410k wishlists. The winner still fills this, at a commercial score of 6.5 rather than 9, and the plan budgets for multiplayer bugs dominating negative reviews, which is what Survivalist's and HumanitZ's reviews show.
2. **"The world persists while the host is offline" (confirmed cheap, no longer a differentiator).** [Bellwright](https://pindrop.gg/bellwright/guides/multiplayer), Forever Skies and Len's Island are host-only and [State of Decay 2's host-only co-op (guests cannot build or claim, 500 m tether, host-only progress)](https://steamcommunity.com/app/495420/discussions/0/4338734550481158233) is the pre-mortem; but [HumanitZ ships dedicated servers up to 24 players with persistent offline worlds](https://www.survivalservers.com/services/game_servers/humanitz/), so this is now the lane's baseline. The implementation exists: steamworks-rs GameServer sockets ([PR #249](https://github.com/Noxime/steamworks-rs/pull/249)), aeronet_steam's Server handle ([PR #94](https://github.com/aecsocket/aeronet/pull/94), release-candidate only) or a 20-40 line [renet2_steam](https://github.com/UkoeHB/renet2/blob/main/renet2_steam/src/server.rs) patch; Valve's [SDK header](https://github.com/Noxime/steamworks-rs/blob/master/steamworks-sys/lib/steam/public/steam/isteamnetworkingsockets.h) permits P2P listen sockets on a dedicated server "without a ticket"; [Core Keeper ships exactly this](https://github.com/escapingnetwork/core-keeper-dedicated) (relay by default, no port forwarding, no login token). Cost: days of glue plus a one-day spike, not 100-300 hours; relay mode is Steam-only, so the zero-cost promise applies to the Steam SKU.
3. **Settlement/NPC colony layer on survival.** [Bellwright 1M](https://www.gamespress.com/Medieval-Survival-RPG-Bellwright-Surpasses-1-Million-Units-Sold-on-Ste), [Necesse 2M](https://massivelyop.com/2025/11/09/sandbox-mmo-lite-necesse-has-sold-an-eye-popping-2-million-copies-so-far/), Soulmask 1M, Dinkum 1.4M; it is the founder's strongest pillar.
4. **The Division fantasy on PC (supplied at AAA scale; demoted).** [ARC Raiders (Embark/Nexon, 16.3M copies, 481,966 Steam peak)](https://tweaktown.com/news/111632/arc-raiders-hits-16-million-sales-helps-drive-record-revenue-and-profits-for-nexon/index.html) and [The Division 2's own 27,482 Steam record in 2026](https://insider-gaming.com/the-division-2-player-count-soar-after-roadmap/) falsify "unsupplied" as stated; [Heartland was cancelled and Resurgence sits at 46%](https://www.shanethegamer.com/pc-mac/the-division-resurgence-lands-on-steam-splits-players-with-46-positive-score/). The unsupplied variant is small-team, PvE-only with a settlement layer; served inside the winner by the Meltline zone, not as the headline, and no longer used as a pitch argument.
5. **Persistent server with many cities on one world, polished.** Only [Eco (~683k, 74.9h, 7+ years EA)](https://github.com/VIV-T/BUT3_Developpement_web/tree/64d0a457f8f9c5a159c424cd2d9974d0764d4e9b/1_Preparation_Donnees/donneesBrutes/scrapping_gamesData_Gamalytics) supplies it; communities build it themselves ([RimWorld Together](https://github.com/RimWorld-Together/Rimworld-Together), [TimberTogether](https://github.com/timbermods/TimberTogether)). Demoted by the skeptics: the one solo paid comp of the async shape, [Ymir, has 1,079 reviews at 76% after 7.5 years in EA](https://store.steampowered.com/app/378360/Ymir/). Served as a post-launch meta-layer.
6. **Rise of Kingdoms without pay-to-win on PC.** [196 RoK repos, mostly grind bots](https://github.com/Dylan-Zheng/Rise-of-Kingdoms-Bot), are a design brief; but the audience lives on mobile and no paid PC comp exists.
7. **"No desync" as a selling point.** Every shipped multiplayer builder was punished for netcode ([Kingdoms Reborn](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1307890/discussions/0/3823048293511738403/)); it is a review-risk mitigator, not a purchase driver.

### 6.1 Longthaw (second pass 8.83; post-review 7.33)

**Hook and loop.** Hallam County ten years after a volcanic winter; each real week the thaw exposes new ruins and floods others on a reseeded twelve-week season. Out: 30-60 minute runs into kit-built ruins, typed junk under a deadweight meter, slow telegraphed top-down combat, a meltwater clock that runs only in-session. Home: settlers as visible agents with a printed ledger; progress persists 24/7 on a dedicated server or catches up deterministically on a listen server. The Meltline (90-second pump extraction, drop-on-death) is the opt-in Dark Zone; the County pins hearths to a shared map for caravans, compacts of 20, a seasonal Great Work, visitable snapshots and phone check-ins.

**Multiplayer model.** One sim crate, three deployments: embedded listen server (1-4), free dedicated binary (up to 8), a County meta-service syncing kilobytes per hearth per hour; Steam Datagram Relay and lobbies, friends-list only, no matchmaking.

**Comparables and market evidence (revised 2026-10-07).** The nearest shipped products were absent from the pitch. [Project Zomboid](https://raijin.gg/app/108600/Project_Zomboid): ~448,905 reviews at 94%; copies-sold estimates span 7.9M to 17.9M by estimator; B42 multiplayer went unstable on [2025-12-11, twelve months after B42 single-player, and stable on 2026-07-29 with "20 players or fewer, disable mods, back up saves" warnings and a stability hotfix five weeks later](https://shockbyte.com/blog/project-zomboid-build-42-multiplayer); [121,603 peak on 2026-08-02 against 65,505 in January 2022](https://nichegamer.com/project-zomboid-breaks-its-steam-record-again-with-over-121000-players/); [price raised to $24.99](https://finalboss.io/project-zomboids-120k-steam-record-comes-with-a-higher-price-tag). [HumanitZ](https://raijin.gg/app/1766060/HumanitZ): isometric, 1-4 co-op plus dedicated servers, $19.99, a first-time team of unconfirmed size plus Freedom Games; [EA 2023-09-18 at a ~1,512 peak](https://shockbyte.com/blog/shockbyte-success-story-humanitz-launch-and-community), [1.0 on 2026-02-06 requiring a new save](https://www.indie.io/post/sandbox-zombie-survival-humanitz-exits-steam-early-access-on-feb-6), [12,674 peak on 2026-02-15 then a monthly average falling 5,733 to 1,283 by June, -78% in four months](https://steambase.io/games/humanitz/steam-charts); est. 245k copies, $2.9M gross, ~410k wishlists (Raijin, medium). [CryoFall](https://www.steampulse.org/game/829590): [five developers, not two](https://atomictorch.com/Pages/FAQ), Daedalic, in-house engine, [EA 2019-04-03 to 1.0 2021-04-29](https://rpgamer.com/2021/04/cryofall-fully-launching-next-week/), 6,414 reviews at 78%, est. 192k units and $2.2M. [7 Days to Die](https://thefunpimps.com/): 20M+ copies (official), [398,091 reviews at 87%, 125,419 peak at 1.0 after eleven years of EA](https://steambase.io/apps/251570). [State of Decay 2](https://raijin.gg/app/495420/State_of_Decay_2): 69,487 reviews at 83%, [host-only co-op (guests cannot build or claim, 500 m tether, host-only progress)](https://steamcommunity.com/app/495420/discussions/0/4338734550481158233) called "barebones". [Core Keeper](https://wnhub.io/news/finance/item-45228): 2M at 1.0 with 31k CCU, 4M by October 2025, [65,115 reviews at 93%](https://steambase.io/games/core-keeper/reviews), SDR by default, a 5-to-17-person team plus Fireshine: the top-down co-op ceiling with a differentiated hook and a publisher. Base rates: top-down/isometric survival or base-building with online co-op [10.6% at 1,000 reviews (5/47), 2.9% self-published, 4.3% at 5,000; 3D equivalent 20.2% and 17.6% self-published](https://github.com/leinstay/steamdb/releases/latest/download/steamdb.min.json.gz); the genre-controlled model's prediction for a self-published, first-title, Early Access, $19.99, English-only top-down survival co-op game is 17% at 1,000 and 7% at 5,000, rising to 36% and 18% with eight launch languages (analyst model on the dump, medium); OWSC [20.8%](https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/01/27/what-the-hell-happened-in-2025/) applies to the 3D lane; co-op [8.4x raw, 3.0-4.4x controlled at 5,000 reviews](https://github.com/leinstay/steamdb/releases/latest/download/steamdb.min.json.gz); [$15-19.99 online co-op 18.2% at 1,000](https://github.com/leinstay/steamdb/releases/latest/download/steamdb.min.json.gz); [Abiotic Factor's ~250k-wishlist demo week](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/how-abiotic-factor-sold-600k-copies) and [co-op survival's five-year run at the top of Steam's charts](https://cogconnected.com/2026/07/co-op-survival-games-wont-stop-eating-steams-charts/) are the demand-side evidence that survives.

**12-month scope as pitched.** Q1 sim crate, Bevy client, one kit, 2-player replicon over local UDP, Steam page mid-December, Gate 1 solo-fun test; Q2 SDR lobbies, 4-player drop-in, voice, dedicated binary, three kits, five settler jobs, Deck pass, alpha of 50; Q3 Meltline, two kits, prediction, June Next Fest demo, County v1 as a dark feature, Gate 3 at 15k wishlists; Q4 freeze, Deck Verified, EA late September 2027 at $19.99 with six kits and 20-25 hours; ~2,560 productive hours. *Bottom-up cost (added 2026-10-07; three-point optimistic / likely / pessimistic founder-hours with agents, first-year Rust and Bevy, analyst estimate, low-medium):* Q1 395 / 790 / 1,440 (sim crate 60/120/200, egui viewer 15/30/60, Bevy client skeleton 80/160/280, one kit 40/80/140, combat loop 80/160/300, two-player replicon with hand-rolled interpolation 80/160/320, page and trailer 40/80/140) against ~540-600 logged and ~400-450 productive hours in the box; Q2 400 / 820 / 1,580 (SDR and lobbies 60/140/300, 4-player drop-in 60/120/240, two kits 60/120/200, five settler jobs 100/200/360, Deck pass 40/80/160, 50-person alpha 80/160/320); Q3 320 / 640 / 1,200 plus 0-240 for unpinned Bevy migrations (onboarding 80/160/300, two regions 120/240/450 each). Total ~1,115 / 2,250 / 4,220 against ~1,750-1,950 logged (~1,450-1,650 productive) by month 9: the likely case is 1.4x the box, and at the 0.5-0.6x velocity the comps and METR imply the pitched scope completes around month 15-18. No comp publishes an hour log; converting calendar time at 1,800-2,000 hours per person-year, Survivalist is ~9,000-10,000 solo hours in EA alone, Tiny Glade ~9,000-10,000 person-hours with no netcode, CryoFall ~19,000-21,000, ZERO Sievert ~7,000-8,000 single-player: 1.4-7x the 3,000-hour budget before the Rust ramp.

**Market: refuted (medium).** At the [median 0.10x wishlist-to-week-1 conversion above $10](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/the-state-of-steam-wishlist-conversions), a [2.7x year-one multiplier](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gamediscoverco-games-long-tail-revenue) and [~69% of US list realized](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/tools/regional-pricing-calculator), the 15k gate yields ~1,500 week-one units and ~$50k gross (~$30k net); $100k gross needs ~30k wishlists, while a new page gets [100-1,200 in two weeks](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/blog/how-many-wishlists-before-launch) and the [median June 2026 Next Fest demo gained ~200](https://wnhub.io/news/stores-and-publishing/item-51221). Every solo comp that cleared $100k in year one had years of pre-launch work plus a publisher ([ZERO Sievert with Modern Wolf, 277,966 wishlists at launch](https://gameworldobserver.com/tag/cabo-studio)). *Mitigation adopted (revised 2026-10-07):* 25-30k-wishlist or publisher gate; a public demo of the main game on its own store page three to four months before the one permitted Next Fest (February 2028 by default; October 2027 as a withdrawable option), no Prologue app (a prologue is ineligible as a Next Fest entrant, the main page may not cross-promote it since August 2024, and its wishlists convert weakly: [WarriOrb's 16k prologue wishlists became 357 copies in three days](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/tales-from-discoverabilityland-when); [Tinkerlands](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/press-release/tinkerlands-early-access-launches-on-may-23-new-demo-available-now-), the earlier precedent, released its prologue before the July 2024 demo overhaul and entered Next Fest with the main game's demo); EA inside the ~30-day post-fest halo, realistically March-May 2028; publisher talks at Gate 2. The gate buys a Survivalist/CryoFall-shaped result; the HumanitZ-shaped ceiling came with a publisher and ~410k wishlists, roughly 15x the gate.

**Ship: refuted (high for the 9-12 month EA; the 3-month prototype survives).** No solo or tiny-team comp reached EA with less scope in under two years ([ZERO Sievert ~2 years solo, single-player](https://gameworldobserver.com/2023/01/23/zero-sievert-1-million-revenue-cabo-studio-extraction-shooter-steam); [Valheim ~4 years, 5 people](https://gamesbeat.com/valheim-finally-hits-1-0-after-five-years-in-early-access-interview/); [Tiny Glade ~2.5 years, 2 people, Bevy, no multiplayer](https://80.lv/articles/relaxing-castle-builder-tiny-glade-has-finally-arrived-on-steam/)); the agent multiplier is contradicted by [METR](https://metr.org/blog/2025-07-10-early-2025-ai-experienced-os-dev-study/); and [renet_steam pins steamworks 0.12.2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lucaspoffo/renet/master/renet_steam/Cargo.toml) while [bevy_steamworks 0.17 pins 0.13.1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HouraiTeahouse/bevy_steamworks/main/Cargo.toml), two incompatible Client types. The gap pass sharpened the netcode finding: the version split is resolved by [renet2_steam 0.17.1 (steamworks ^0.13)](https://github.com/UkoeHB/renet2/blob/main/renet2_steam/src/server.rs), but renet_steam, renet2_steam and lightyear_steam all construct the server transport from a Steam `Client`, which is null on a headless box, so the dedicated binary needs [aeronet_steam 0.22's Server handle](https://github.com/aecsocket/aeronet/pull/94) (release candidate) or a 20-40 line renet2_steam patch, and [bevy_replicon has no built-in interpolation or prediction](https://github.com/projectharmonia/bevy_replicon), so the Q1 two-player line includes bevy_replicon_snap or hand-rolled interpolation. The calendar also fails on hours: the Q1 scope is ~790 likely hours into a ~400-450 productive-hour box, and the two-player loop realistically lands at week 18-24. *Mitigation adopted (revised):* year-one build = hearth with three settler jobs + one kit and one region + one enemy family + 2-player co-op over plain UDP, ~1,800-2,000 hours including Steam overhead; 4-player and SDR behind the week-8 gate; dedicated binary at EA day one if the pre-week-1 spike passes, else EA+60 days; County at EA+6 months; 18 months to EA as base plan; 45-50 hour cap yielding 2,025-2,250 logged hours; numeric week-8 velocity gate (Section 7); Bevy pinned to 0.20; one transport path with a CI check on duplicate steamworks versions; week-6 Gate 0 on the ant farm.

**MP model: refuted (high on anti-cheat; medium on population).** County inputs are HMAC-signed receipts from a server the player owns and can patch, so a receipt proves nothing; every client-held economy has been flooded ([Diablo II open Battle.net](https://diablo2.diablowiki.net/B.net), [Fallout 76 dupes](https://primagames.com/news/duped-items-deleted-new-fallout-76-patch), [ARK's wipe three weeks after launch](https://massivelyop.com/2015/06/23/excessive-duping-leads-to-ark-survival-evolved-wipe/)); free-text mail makes a solo developer a user-to-user service under the [UK Online Safety Act](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/online-safety/the-online-safety-act-and-gaming-know-the-risks-know-the-rules-know-how-to-comply); [steamworks-rs has no voice module](https://github.com/Noxime/steamworks-rs/issues?q=voice). *Mitigation adopted:* the County mints its own credit on a fixed per-hearth schedule and only debits what it minted; Great Works score participation, never quantity; compacts of 8-10; no free text; opens above ~300 hearths; voice dropped; version handshake for dedicated servers.

**Net assessment (revised).** Longthaw stays the recommendation, re-scored 6.5 / 7 / 8.5 = 7.33 and tied with Ashward: its Phase 1 is the one multiplayer model the evidence supports, the lane is the strongest on Steam even though it is occupied, and every mitigation is a cut or a gate rather than a new system. Commercial drops from 9 to 6.5 because the slot's real outcome distribution is Survivalist/CryoFall-shaped with a HumanitZ ceiling and the tag set's base rate is 10.6% (2.9% self-published) rather than 20.8%; feasibility drops from 9 to 7 because the pitched Q1-Q3 scope costs 1.4x the hours available while the staged ~2,000-hour build fits at 0.8-1.0x velocity and the SDR server path is confirmed as glue; founder-fit holds at 8.5 (it is the loop he plays most and the GameServer binding is a fine Rust curriculum), with the lane-specific burnout exposure named in Section 7. Three things in the pitch are retired: the "Duckov lacks multiplayer" and "Division unsupplied on PC" arguments, the Prologue app, and the 2,560-hour calendar. The year-one build is the Haulback-shaped slice of Longthaw; the full pitch is the upside case if the week-8 velocity gate reads 0.8 or better.

**Market: refuted (medium).** Developer net per $19.99 unit is [~$8-9](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/blog/steam-revenue-share-explained), so $100k net needs ~11,500-12,500 units and the 7-15k gate yields $10-60k net at [0.07-0.13x EA conversion and 2.46-3.6x year one](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/what-long-tail-should-you-expect); in the catalog, self-published entrants of this shape (N=20) have a median of 311 reviews and first-time self-published ones (N=5) a median of 13; [Nested Lands (veteran team plus publisher) sits at an est. $105.9k gross after 7 months](https://raijin.gg/app/2553040/Nested_Lands); [Romestead had 250k wishlists and a publisher before an est. $1.1M in 8 weeks](https://raijin.gg/app/1805320/Romestead). *Mitigation (revised):* 25k gate, a demo of the main game in the one permitted Next Fest (October 2027 at the earliest, February 2028 by default) with the dam-drain as the trailer shot, no Prologue app, EA Q1-Q2 2028, publisher talks at 5k. The same occupied-lane evidence as Longthaw applies (Zomboid, HumanitZ, Survivalist, CryoFall in 6.1).

**Ship: refuted (high).** ~2,560 hours is below [ZERO Sievert's ~4,000 for the single-player half alone](https://howtomarketagame.com/2023/01/18/how-a-pixel-art-shooter-achieved-a-million-dollar-launch-the-zero-sievert-story/) (the ~4,000-hour figure could not be re-verified in the gap pass because howtomarketagame.com was blocked; the verifiable record is ~4 years solo from a 2020 studio founding to 1.0 on 2024-10-23, medium) and 15-20x below [Abiotic Factor's ~2.5 years with 10-12 people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abiotic_Factor); [Sapiens, the nearest settler-sim-plus-multiplayer analogue, took a decade solo](https://majic-jungle.prezly.com/sapiens-press-kit); [Bithell was hospitalized a year into full-time indie work](https://www.mcvuk.com/development-news/how-mike-bithells-never-ending-crunch-on-volume-sent-him-to-hospital/). *Mitigation:* EA at month 15-18 gated on a month-9 demo with 7k+ wishlists; 2-3 kits; interpolation-only combat; freeze Bevy; Board in year 2.

**MP model: refuted (medium-high).** Save-editing tools appear within weeks for every player-hosted game ([palworld-save-tools, 869 stars](https://github.com/cheahjs/palworld-save-tools); [valheim-save-tools](https://github.com/Kakoen/valheim-save-tools)), so Board inputs are attacker-controlled and "never wiped" removes the only remedy; the dual build is the shape of [Palworld's host-save GUID bug (945 stars, 68 open issues)](https://github.com/xNul/palworld-host-save-fix); [LFGSS shut down rather than carry OSA liability](https://www.techdirt.com/company/microcosm/). *Mitigation:* Board-minted scrip with daily caps, cooperative thresholds, preset-phrase mail, Steam ID as sole identity, 2-3 h/week of named ops.

**Net assessment (revised).** Re-scored 6 / 6.5 / 7.5 = 6.67: Longthaw in the same occupied lane with a stronger single image, more shader and water-simulation work in founder hours, and weaker gates; the reservoir lever is worth stealing as a Season-2 Great Work.

### 6.3 Ashward (second pass 7.67; post-review 7.33)

**Hook, loop and model.** A burned-over timber valley regreening tile by tile as 48 player towns plus NPC towns grow; a town is an agent-simulated diorama with a ledger and shortfall forecast; salvage parties resolve on printed odds; one valley day per 10 real minutes; fire season and winter as shared pressure; great works need several towns; towns can never be lost. Phase 2 (H1 2028): the adjacent ruin hex becomes a 20 Hz 1-4 player instance, the Burn its contested zone. Developer-hosted shards from day one (one Rust process per 48-town valley, WebSocket over axum, event-sourced Postgres) plus a free private-valley binary with a configurable tick.

**Comparables and market evidence.** [Eco ~683k / 74.9h](https://github.com/VIV-T/BUT3_Developpement_web/tree/64d0a457f8f9c5a159c424cd2d9974d0764d4e9b/1_Preparation_Donnees/donneesBrutes/scrapping_gamesData_Gamalytics), Kingdoms Reborn ~572k, [Foxhole 44,068 reviews](https://github.com/leinstay/steamdb), [OpenFront's 190k wishlists](https://github.com/openfrontio/OpenFrontIO/blob/main/resources/public/press/index.html), the [4X tag's 29.7-36.8% hit rate on 19-49 releases](https://github.com/leinstay/steamdb/releases/latest/download/steamdb.min.json.gz) (2026-10-04 snapshot), [Massively Multiplayer's $6.8M top-100 median](https://vginsights.com/) (VG Insights homepage; search snippet, unverified).

**12-month scope.** Q1 plain-Rust valley sim plus tokio/axum/sqlx server, egui viewer, Bevy 2D client, five friends on a 60x dev valley, page mid-December; Q2 NPC towns, market, caravans, great work, private-valley binary, phone web client, alpha of 50-100; Q3 Steam auth, Deck pass, Valley API, bot load tests, Next Fest demo valley; Q4 EA late September 2027 at $19.99 with two official valleys, Phase 2 spike. One new stack per quarter.

**Market: refuted (medium).** The shipped product is a slow-tick 2D async town game whose one solo precedent, [Ymir (solo, $34.99, EA since 2019-03-16), has 1,079 reviews at 76% and is still in EA after 7.5 years](https://store.steampowered.com/app/378360/Ymir/) at an est. [$594k lifetime](https://github.com/leinstay/steamdb); [Screeps is est. $570k-$1.45M over ten years](https://raijin.gg/app/464350/Screeps_World); [simulogics called the Prosperous Universe subscription a "necessity"](https://massivelyop.com/?p=311532); paid "Asynchronous Multiplayer" releases have a median of 28 reviews. *Mitigation:* make the EA SKU a co-op town builder for 1-4 friends on private valleys with official valleys second; put a 10-15 minute embodied salvage slice in the Next Fest demo; gate at 25k wishlists or 2k sustained browser DAU; budget $40-80k net.

**Ship: refuted (medium-high; the 3-month slice survives).** The niche has [82 persistent-server strategy/builder titles in the catalog, 37 paid, 7 ever at 1,000 reviews](https://github.com/leinstay/steamdb); [parabellum is 22 months into its Rust rewrite and incomplete](https://github.com/andreapavoni/parabellum); [OpenFront took 25 months and 279 commit authors](https://github.com/openfrontio/OpenFrontIO); [OpenMMO's 2,622 commits include 1,619 co-authored by Claude](https://github.com/Julian-adv/OpenMMO), which proves an agent-assisted solo server is possible but not that fun in a 10-minutes-a-day valley is found faster, since that takes real-speed valley years with humans. *Mitigation:* ship the private valley in year one; replace the wishlist gate with a fun gate (three friend groups complete a valley year and start another); official valleys in year two.

**MP model: refuted (medium-high).** Fixed 48 permanent slots, no wipes and multi-town great works cannot all hold once Steam decay arrives: [Eco shows 26 reviews in the last 30 days against 13,212 lifetime, Kingdoms Reborn 33 of 9,113, BitCraft 51 of 3,757](https://github.com/leinstay/steamdb), so 48 humans become 5-10 plus 40 frozen ghost towns; every long-lived slow-tick game broke one of these promises ([Screeps' novice and respawn areas](https://docs.screeps.com/start-areas.html)); the genre is the most-botted on GitHub ([214 "travian bot" repos](https://github.com/Erol444/TravianBotSharp), [140 "ogame bot"](https://github.com/ogame-tbot/TBot)); [Hetzner dedicated datacenters are Germany and Finland only](https://github.com/elsbrock/hetzner-radar). *Mitigation:* 12-week valley seasons with a persistent hearth carried forward; dormancy after 14 days with the town held in trust by NPCs; elastic valleys opening at 12-16 humans; an attention budget identical for every town; Steam-ownership-only official valleys; a moderation and OSA/DSA floor; a US-region box.

**Net assessment (revised).** Re-scored 5 / 8 / 9 = 7.33, tied with Longthaw on the unweighted average and the named fallback. Feasibility is now relatively stronger than Longthaw's because its stack (tokio, axum, sqlx, Postgres, an engine-free sim) is where agents are nearest 1.3x, and it carries no combat netcode, no SDR and no Bevy-churn exposure on the server; the hours arithmetic in Section 7 still cuts its year by 10-20%. Commercial does not rise: the [Colony Sim 1,000-review rate is 1.0-1.5%](https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/01/27/what-the-hell-happened-in-2025/), the controlled co-op lift of 2-3x puts a 1-4 player valley at roughly 2-4%, below Longthaw's 7-12%, and the one solo comp ([Ymir](https://store.steampowered.com/app/378360/Ymir/)) is unchanged. It remains the best plan if the founder decides not to ship combat, and the deepest Rust curriculum; its dormancy-and-seasons design and one-stack-per-quarter sequencing are adopted; and it is the numerically triggered alternative: V < 0.6 at week 8, or Gate 0 failing, moves the year to Ashward's settlement-only shape on the same simulation crate.

### 6.4 DEADWEIGHT (second pass 6.33; post-review 5.33)

**Hook, loop and model.** "Escape from Duckov meets Valheim" in a drowned container port: every run is a bet on how much you can carry before the tide comes in; sealed cargo is winched at a noisy crane; range-limited proximity voice; a settlement on timers. Player-hosted 1-4 co-op at $17.99, EA at month 12. Comparables: [Duckov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Escape_from_Duckov), [ZERO Sievert](https://underdog.ltd/presskit.html), [Lethal Company 487,160 reviews, solo](https://vginsights.com/game/1966720); [co-op median 27 vs PvP-only 9 reviews](https://github.com/leinstay/steamdb). Scope: month 3 sim crate, lightyear over SDR, one district, deadweight, tide, boat; month 6 four players, three districts, settlers on a dedicated server, crane, voice; month 9 Next Fest demo, refinery, Docker; month 12 EA with five districts.

**Market: refuted (medium).** Paid + Online Co-op + EA + $15-19.99 + self-published 2024 launches (n=33) have a median of 139 reviews and 12% at 1,000; at [HumanitZ's $213-396 gross per review](https://gamalytic.com/game/1766060) (two estimators; see 6.1), $100k gross needs 250-470 reviews in 12 months, a 70th-85th percentile outcome, and HumanitZ itself (5,693 reviews in the dump, 11,100-13,600 on trackers; est. $2.25-2.9M) is a first-time team of unconfirmed size plus a publisher after ~2.5 years; drowned-city comps are weak ([Floodland 1,001 reviews at 56%](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2080690)); [Bevy's sixth-birthday post lists nine shipped titles with 2-60 reviews each](https://bevyengine.org/news/bevys-sixth-birthday/). *Mitigation (revised):* page by month 3, a demo in the one permitted Next Fest, EA only at 20-25k wishlists, plan on 5-10k copies; the Duckov demand pool it was built on has drained (Section 3.3).

**Ship: refuted (high).** [lightyear shipped ten breaking releases between July 2025 and September 2026](https://github.com/cBournhonesque/lightyear/releases); [Steam headless mode was broken from 2025-08-12 to 2026-08-03](https://github.com/cBournhonesque/lightyear/issues/1156) and [Steam IO beside UDP IO silently disables server inputs](https://github.com/cBournhonesque/lightyear/issues/1693); [no Rust voice bindings exist](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Noxime/steamworks-rs/master/src/user.rs); [Bevy system order is nondeterministic by default](https://github.com/bevy-cheatbook/bevy-cheatbook/blob/main/src/programming/system-order.md); [Veloren is at 0.18 after eight years](https://github.com/veloren/veloren). *Mitigation:* pin Bevy 0.20, replicon with interpolation only, drop lightyear, voice and walking settlers from EA, EA Q1 2028.

**MP model: refuted (medium).** The 24/7 promise exists only on the dedicated binary, a second product ([Palworld's Docker image still logs join failures after updates](https://github.com/thijsvanloef/palworld-server-docker/issues/947)); [aeronet_steam ships its session config marked "CURRENTLY BROKEN"](https://github.com/aecsocket/aeronet/blob/main/crates/aeronet_steam/src/config.rs) (the gap pass found this is confined to config-option passing, blocked on [steamworks-rs #169](https://github.com/Noxime/steamworks-rs/issues/169); the transport itself runs on Steam defaults); most friend groups run the listen server. *Mitigation:* offline progression as a deterministic event-timer ledger identical on any load; world save in Steam Cloud with host handoff; dedicated binary as a post-EA milestone.

**Net assessment (revised).** Re-scored 5.5 / 5.5 / 5 = 5.33. The most streamable design and cleanest hosting model, but it spends founder hours on tide shaders, voice FFI and density, drops the shared-world pillars, and was pitched into a Duckov-sized demand pool that had decayed ~97% from peak by July 2026. Its tide clock and "progress between sessions, not 24/7" framing are folded into the recommendation; the pitch stays fourth.

## 7. Recommendation

**Concept: Longthaw, staged.** A $17.99-19.99 1-4 player scavenging game with a settlement that progresses between sessions, in a county that thaws week by week on a reseeded 12-week season. Year one builds the ~2,000-hour slice, the scope Haulback pitched under Longthaw's hook: the hearth with three settler jobs and a printed ledger, one ruin kit and one region, one enemy family, slow telegraphed combat, 2-player co-op over the Rust authoritative server, a Steam page, a 30-90 minute demo, and the thaw calendar visible from the first screenshot. Staged behind gates: 4-player drop-in and the SDR dedicated binary (targeted for EA day one if the pre-week-1 spike passes, since HumanitZ's dedicated-servers-with-persistent-worlds is now the lane's baseline); a second and third kit and the Meltline (90-second pump extraction, drop-on-death) as EA updates; the County at EA+6 months in its mitigated form (it mints its own credit on a fixed per-hearth schedule and only debits what it minted, scores Great Works by participation with no leaderboard, caps compacts at 8-10, carries no free text and opens only above ~300 pinned hearths). In-game Steam voice is not promised; offline progression is a deterministic catch-up function identical on listen and dedicated servers, marketed as "your base progresses between sessions"; open hearths for strangers are deferred indefinitely. The three cheapest differentiators in this lane come straight from [HumanitZ's](https://vaporlens.app/app/1766060/humanit_z) and [Survivalist's](https://www.steamspy.com/app/1054510) negative reviews and belong in founder hours, not agent hours: melee and aim that visibly connect, building that does not live in nested menus, and co-op saves that never lose a guest's inventory or vehicle. Pitch arguments retired: "Duckov and ZERO Sievert lack multiplayer" and "the Division fantasy is unsupplied on PC"; the defensible claim is that no small-team, PvE-only co-op scavenger with a persistent settlement and a calendar hook exists, stated as absence of supply. The name needs a Steam, trademark and domain search before the page goes live.

**Engine.** Bevy client plus Bevy headless server sharing one engine-free simulation crate, with four rules. (1) Pin Bevy 0.20 for the year (rc.2 shipped [2026-09-28](https://crates.io/api/v1/crates/bevy); [0.17 carried 127 migration guides](https://github.com/bevyengine/bevy/tree/v0.17.0/release-content/migration-guides) and 0.18 89, and 2-3 breaking releases land inside the plan window with 2-8 weeks of ecosystem lag each), upgrade at most once before EA at ~40-120 hours, skip the rest until after launch; agents trained on older APIs generate code for the wrong Bevy, so the pinned version's migration guides go in the agent context. (2) Keep the simulation crate free of Bevy types and the wire protocol engine-agnostic (postcard), so the Rust curriculum lives in sim, server, replication and persistence; Godot 4.7 via gdext remains the fallback client if two players cannot walk a ruin together by month 4. (3) Netcode: bevy_replicon for server-authoritative replication; it has [no built-in prediction, interpolation or rollback](https://github.com/projectharmonia/bevy_replicon), so bevy_replicon_snap supplies interpolation, combat is tuned for 80-150 ms, and there is no prediction in year one. The Steam transport is chosen by a one-day spike before week 1, not by reading. Two off-the-shelf routes exist: (a) bevy_replicon_renet2 + renet2_steam 0.17.1 (steamworks ^0.13, the same as bevy_steamworks 0.17) with a 20-40 line patch so `SteamServerTransport` accepts a `steamworks::Server` as well as a `Client` (the `Client` is otherwise used only for FriendsOnly/InLobby checks in [renet2_steam's server.rs](https://github.com/UkoeHB/renet2/blob/main/renet2_steam/src/server.rs); UkoeHB merged four Steam PRs between 2026-09-18 and 2026-10-05, so upstreaming it is realistic and is the founder's first ecosystem contribution); or (b) aeronet_replicon + aeronet_steam 0.22 with `SteamworksSockets::Server` ([PR #94](https://github.com/aecsocket/aeronet/pull/94)), which already ships a dedicated-server example but exists only on the Bevy 0.20 release-candidate line pinned to a bevy_replicon git rev. Default (a); re-evaluate (b) when Bevy 0.20 and aeronet 0.22 are both stable, and use the same IO layer for the listen server (Client variant) and the dedicated binary (Server variant). Not lightyear: its Steam path is still Client-only and it [shipped ten breaking releases in 14 months](https://github.com/cBournhonesque/lightyear/releases). The spike, verbatim from the crates: steamworks-rs [examples/game-server](https://github.com/Noxime/steamworks-rs/blob/master/examples/game-server/src/main.rs) (`Server::init` with `ServerMode::AuthenticationAndSecure`, `set_dedicated_server(true)`, `log_on_anonymous()`, `networking_utils().init_relay_network_access()`, `networking_sockets().create_listen_socket_p2p(0, ..)`) on app ID 480 on a Hetzner box with no Steam client, then `Client::init_app(480)` + `connect_p2p(NetworkingIdentity::new_steam_id(id), 0)` from a desktop. Success means Gap #2 is glue; failure means the dedicated binary ships as raw UDP with port forwarding (the Bellwright-class UX) until it is fixed, written into the plan as a known cost. One steamworks version pinned workspace-wide with a CI job failing on duplicates; renet2 can run the raw-UDP listener beside the Steam one for LAN and non-Steam builds (relay mode is Steam-only and a non-Steam build exposes the server IP). Avian over rapier; no wtransport, WebTransport or WASM on the critical path. (4) Determinism specified: fixed-point economy math, explicitly ordered systems, no float crossing a platform boundary. Learning rule unchanged: the founder hand-writes the first version of every novel subsystem (sim core, replication, persistence, catch-up, the Steam GameServer binding) and reads every agent diff there; agents own tests, fixtures, bots, CI, tooling, the localization pipeline and the County service. Model agents at 1.0-1.3x on backend, protocol and tests and 1.0x on feel, Bevy integration and art until measured ([METR 2025: experienced developers 19% slower on familiar code; the 2026 follow-up's -18% task time carries a -38% to +9% interval that METR calls unreliable](https://blog.robbowley.net/2026/04/04/metrs-developer-productivity-research-2026-update/), secondary source; the primary at metr.org was blocked).

**Platforms.** Steam first: Windows, Steam Deck "Playable" at EA via Proton (gamepad UI from the first quarter, Vulkan from wgpu) with Verified when Valve's queue allows, native Linux as a near-free bonus. No web client, mobile app or console in the first 12 months; a plain-HTML County page for phone check-ins when the County opens. Localization is a platform item, not a polish item: in the dump, co-op hits ship a median of 12 languages against 3 for co-op non-hits, and the genre-controlled model roughly doubles the predicted odds for Longthaw's profile (17% to 36% at 1,000 reviews, 7% to 18% at 5,000) with eight launch languages; language count is partly a consequence of success, so treat it as the cheapest visible lever rather than a guarantee, and budget an agent-drafted, human-checked eight-language pass before the demo.

**Art direction.** The camera is now a commercial decision, not only a cost one. In the dump, self-published 3D (first/third-person) survival co-op reaches 1,000 reviews [17.6% of the time against 2.9-6.8% for top-down and 2D](https://github.com/leinstay/steamdb/releases/latest/download/steamdb.min.json.gz), the Top-Down tag carries a ~20% odds penalty after controls while 3D carries a ~10% premium (not significant), and the OWSC 20-25% hit rate that drove the choice applies to the 3D lane. Default: a third-person follow camera (Valheim-class, over the shoulder, with slow telegraphed combat that tolerates interpolation-only netcode) on one or two low-poly modular kits (Synty/Kenney class) recolored to one palette, with the top-down diorama kept as the hearth's management camera so the ant farm stays legible. Cost: an estimated 100-200 extra founder hours for character controller, camera and animation integration (analyst estimate, low confidence), tested in the week-8 prototype and locked at the art-direction lock; if third-person combat does not feel right by week 8 with interpolation only, fall back to the top-down diorama and accept the lower base rate, pricing accordingly. The thaw is the look either way (white and slate in week one, ochre and green by week twelve), with variation from procedural placement, snowpack depth, meltwater level and a weathering shader; one commissioned settler rig so the ant farm has charm; one commissioned capsule and key art set. The capsule and first screenshot must carry the calendar hook, because that is what makes $19.99 defensible beside Zomboid at $24.99 with thirteen years of content and HumanitZ at $19.99 and 74%. Mod surface stops at data tables because pack licenses forbid redistributing meshes. Year-one cash under $8k.

**Monetization.** Premium only, Valve as merchant of record. Price is argued from the $15-19.99 band, where [paid online co-op reaches 1,000 reviews 18.2% of the time (self-published 18.0%; 5.8% at 5,000)](https://github.com/leinstay/steamdb/releases/latest/download/steamdb.min.json.gz), not from the $20-29.99 band's 36-48%, which the regression shows is mostly games that could already charge more (log-price odds ratio 2.6-3.2 after controls). $19.99 at EA if the Gate 3 capsule test shows the hook lands; $17.99 if it does not; $24.99 at 1.0, the ladder [Zomboid itself climbed](https://finalboss.io/project-zomboids-120k-steam-record-comes-with-a-higher-price-tag); never below $15 (sub-$15 online co-op reaches 1,000 reviews 5-6% of the time). Supporter edition and cosmetics after 1.0; no F2P, subscription, timers or wipes. Honest per-unit model: ~$12.40 gross and ~$7.40 net at $19.99, so $100k gross is ~8,000 year-one units and needs ~30k launch wishlists at median conversion; dollars per review in this lane are $213-396 (HumanitZ, two estimators), so a Survivalist-shaped 3,000-review outcome is roughly $0.6-1.2M lifetime over several years and a HumanitZ-shaped one ~$3M, and the median case is what the founder should plan cash around.

**Multiplayer model.** Listen server embedded in the host's client (solo is the same server on localhost); free headless dedicated binary on SDR through the GameServer interface with a P2P listen socket (Valve's "auto-ticket" mode: no coordinator, no certificate, no ticket generator); [Core Keeper's join-by-Game-ID UX](https://github.com/escapingnetwork/core-keeper-dedicated) with opt-in direct IP; operators may paste a free Game Server Login Token so a dedicated server's ID survives restarts, anonymous logon otherwise; friends-list invites only, no matchmaking, nothing that needs anyone else online. The binary is a separate tool app ID distributed through anonymous SteamCMD ([Valve's game-server distribution doc](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/sdk/uploading/distributing_gs), snippet, medium). The County is a low-tick meta-service syncing kilobytes per hearth per hour and never running simulation.

**Hosting cost.** Simulation: $0 (player-hosted; SDR is free to Steam partners, medium confidence: the partner pages were blocked and the claim rests on Valve's SDK headers, the GameNetworkingSockets README and search snippets). The exception is the Next Fest week: a demo whose co-op runs on the host's machine costs nothing, while a developer-hosted demo at unknown concurrency (top demos exceed 4,000 CCU) means a week of autoscaled boxes and on-call for one person, so the demo's co-op is the listen server. County: one Hetzner cloud or AX-class box at roughly EUR 15-50 per month with Postgres on-box, offsite backups ~$5 per month, domain $15 per year, under $700 for the year (provider prices unverified, low confidence). For scale, the [AWS persistent-world reference costs $1.80 per CCU-month at 1,500 CCU with 64% egress](https://github.com/aws-solutions-library-samples/guidance-for-persistent-world-game-hosting-on-aws); the derived Hetzner-class figure is $0.05-0.50. Founder ops: ~3 hours per week after the County opens, one restore drill per quarter.

**Milestones (Oct 2026 to Sep 2027; 2,025-2,250 logged hours, ~1,600-1,950 productive; item costs are the three-point optimistic / likely / pessimistic founder-hours from 6.1, agents included).**

- *Week 0.* The SDR spike (one day; result written into the plan). Name clearance. Two days re-verifying the key comps at the primary sources: HumanitZ's review count and 1.0 post-mortems, the Zomboid B42 multiplayer Thursdoids, Survivalist's co-op model, the Steamworks Next Fest and themed-sale pages while logged in, and the ZERO Sievert hours interview.
- *Q1, Oct-Dec 2026 (weeks 1-13, ~540-600 logged).* Rust ramp: plan 0.5x for weeks 1-8 and 0.75x for weeks 9-16 ([a third of Google's engineers matched prior-language productivity within two months, just over half by four](https://opensource.googleblog.com/2023/06/rust-fact-vs-fiction-5-insights-from-googles-rust-journey-2022.html)), a 180-300 hour tax. Plain-Rust sim crate with no engine and no async (60/120/200): settlers, jobs, typed components, buildings, ledger, thaw-stage model, fixed step, proptest conservation, versioned serde saves. egui viewer (15/30/60) by week 5; Gate 0 at week 6: is the ant farm fun alone? If not, the settlement is the product and the expedition half is cut. Bevy 0.20 client skeleton (80/160/280), one ruin kit (40/80/140), first combat loop (80/160/300), two-player replicon over plain UDP with bevy_replicon_snap interpolation (80/160/320). Steam page (40/80/140) by week 10-12 with capsule, six screenshots and a 45-second trailer; weekly devlog; Thursday test group from week 2; art direction and camera locked by week 8 from a prototype of both cameras. Q1's likely total is ~790 hours against ~400-450 productive hours in the box, so the two-player loop realistically lands at week 18-24 and the page is the only Q1 item allowed to slip behind the loop.
- *Week 8 velocity gate (numeric).* V = (sum of likely-hours for milestone items fully done) / (logged founder-hours x 0.85), recorded weekly from week 4 so the week-8 number is a trend. V ≥ 0.8: keep the full Longthaw pitch in view and consider the October 2027 Next Fest. 0.6 ≤ V < 0.8: keep the staged scope, defer SDR past EA, take February 2028. V < 0.6, or no end-to-end two-client authoritative session (move, shoot, loot, replicate) by week 10: switch immediately to the ~2,000-hour build and re-baseline, or to Ashward's settlement-only year on the same crate if Gate 0 also failed.
- *Q2, Jan-Mar 2027 (~600-650 logged).* Finish the two-player loop; Gate 1 (a 30-minute run is fun alone for 3 of 4 testers, or pivot to valley-first on the same crate) at month 5-6 rather than month 3.
